# 🏨 P&K Select: Brand Review & Strategic Investment Dashboard

An executive-ready, 2-page Power BI business intelligence solution built for the **Power BI Dataviz World Champs 2027**. 

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### Page 1: Brand Review & Baseline Performance
* **Scope:** Analyzes 2025 financial baselines across 40 urban hospitality properties for **P&K Select**[cite: 1, 2].
* **Key Metrics:** Total Revenue ($1.20B), Net Operating Income ($479M), Occupancy Rate (71.76%), and Guest Satisfaction / NPS (14.76)[cite: 1, 2].
* **Visuals:** Features a 2025 monthly revenue vs. operating expenses trend analysis and a regional performance breakdown[cite: 1, 2].

### Page 2: Investment Strategy & Portfolio Allocation
* **Scope:** Evaluates capital allocation against a **$135M budget ceiling**, presenting a high-ROI 3-project portfolio (`PS-03` Hybrid Meeting Tech, `PS-06` Corporate Sales, and `PS-07` New Austin Hotel)[cite: 2].
* **Key Metrics:** Total Capex Invested ($125.20M), leaving a $9.80M budget buffer, projected to drive **+$31.55M in annual revenue impact** and **+$11.78M in operating profit impact**[cite: 2].
* **Visuals:** Budget allocation breakdown, multi-project return comparison chart, and an executive project summary table.
